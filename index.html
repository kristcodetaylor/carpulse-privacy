AI CarPulse — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 July 2026 · App: AI CarPulse (com.carhealthai.app) · Contact: choxy3004@gmail.com

Hrvatska verzija dolje ↓

1. Who we are

AI CarPulse is a vehicle-health mobile application developed by an independent developer in Croatia. We operate no servers of our own and run no user accounts in the cloud.

2. Data stored only on your device

Everything you record in the app — vehicles, service history, trips, fuel logs, tire data, documents, settings, your name and the assistant's saved notes — is stored locally on your phone. We cannot access it. Deleting the app's data or uninstalling the app permanently removes it.

3. Data sent to third parties (only when you use a feature)

  • AI features (diagnosis from symptoms, photo of a dashboard light, vehicle photo recognition, tire photo check, the Pulse assistant): the text and/or photo you submit, together with basic details of your vehicle (make, model, year, mileage, sensor readings), is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Anthropic (Claude API) to generate the response. Per Anthropic's API terms, API data is not used to train their models. We do not store these requests anywhere.
  • News, weather, radar-announcement and event briefings: the app fetches public feeds (Index.hr, 24sata, N1, Google News RSS, Open-Meteo). For weather and local searches, only the city name you ask about is included in the request.
  • Opening other apps (navigation, music, messaging): the app only opens a link in the app you chose (e.g. a prefilled message you wrote). Nothing is sent anywhere else.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera / photo library — used only when you choose to take or select a photo for an AI feature.
  • Microphone / speech recognition — used only while you talk to the assistant; speech-to-text runs through your device's Google speech service.
  • Location — used on-device for the GPS performance timer. Your location is not transmitted to us or stored.
  • Bluetooth — used only to connect to your OBD-II adapter.

5. What we don't do

No advertising networks. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No sale or sharing of personal data. No server-side profile of you exists.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Because your data lives on your device, you exercise access and deletion directly: view it in the app, delete records in the app, or clear all data by uninstalling. For any privacy question or request, contact choxy3004@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in Croatia: AZOP).

7. Children

The app is intended for drivers and is not directed at children under 16.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.

AI CarPulse — Pravila privatnosti (HR)

Vrijedi od: 10. srpnja 2026. · Aplikacija: AI CarPulse (com.carhealthai.app) · Kontakt: choxy3004@gmail.com

1. Tko smo

AI CarPulse je mobilna aplikacija za praćenje zdravlja vozila neovisnog razvijatelja iz Hrvatske. Nemamo vlastite servere ni korisničke račune u oblaku.

2. Podaci koji ostaju samo na tvom uređaju

Sve što upišeš u aplikaciju — vozila, servisi, putovanja, gorivo, gume, dokumenti, postavke, tvoje ime i bilješke asistenta — pohranjeno je lokalno na tvom telefonu. Mi tome nemamo pristup. Brisanjem podataka aplikacije ili deinstalacijom trajno se uklanjaju.

3. Podaci koji se šalju trećim stranama (samo kad koristiš funkciju)

  • AI funkcije (dijagnoza, fotografija lampice, prepoznavanje vozila, provjera gume, asistent Pulse): tekst i/ili fotografija koju pošalješ, uz osnovne podatke o vozilu, šalje se kriptiranom vezom (HTTPS) tvrtki Anthropic (Claude API) radi generiranja odgovora. Prema Anthropicovim API uvjetima, ti podaci se ne koriste za treniranje modela. Mi ih nigdje ne pohranjujemo.
  • Vijesti, vrijeme, najave kontrola i eventi: aplikacija dohvaća javne izvore (Index.hr, 24sata, N1, Google News RSS, Open-Meteo). Šalje se samo naziv grada koji pitaš.
  • Otvaranje drugih aplikacija (navigacija, glazba, poruke): otvara se samo poveznica u aplikaciji koju si odabrao — ništa se ne šalje drugamo.

4. Dozvole uređaja

  • Kamera / galerija — samo kad sam odabereš fotografiju za AI funkciju.
  • Mikrofon / prepoznavanje govora — samo dok govoriš asistentu; pretvorba govora ide kroz Google servis na tvom uređaju.
  • Lokacija — koristi se na uređaju za GPS mjerenje ubrzanja; ne šalje se nama niti se pohranjuje.
  • Bluetooth — samo za spajanje na tvoj OBD-II adapter.

5. Što NE radimo

Nema reklamnih mreža. Nema analitike ni praćenja. Nema prodaje ni dijeljenja osobnih podataka. Ne postoji nikakav tvoj profil na serveru.

6. Tvoja prava (GDPR)

Budući da podaci žive na tvom uređaju, pristup i brisanje obavljaš izravno: pregledaj ih u aplikaciji, obriši zapise u aplikaciji ili sve ukloni deinstalacijom. Za pitanja o privatnosti piši na choxy3004@gmail.com. Imaš pravo i na pritužbu nadzornom tijelu (u Hrvatskoj: AZOP).

7. Djeca

Aplikacija je namijenjena vozačima i nije usmjerena djeci mlađoj od 16 godina.
